# Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 2026

## Overview

Comedy OS is designed to help stand-up comedians organize their material, build sets, and track performances.

Your privacy is important. This app is built to function without collecting or transmitting personal data.

## Data Collection

Comedy OS does **not collect, store, or share any personal data**.

- No account is required
- No user data is transmitted to external servers
- No analytics or tracking tools are used

All data entered into the app (such as jokes, sets, and gig information) is stored locally on your device.

## Data Storage

Your data remains on your device unless you choose to export or back it up.

You are responsible for managing your own backups.

## Third-Party Services

Comedy OS does not use third-party services that collect user data.
